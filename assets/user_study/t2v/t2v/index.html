Does the video show the actions?

What you will judge

Each task shows one AI-generated video and a list of actions. For each action, answer Yes if it is clearly and fully shown in the video, and No if it is missing, only partly shown, unclear, out of place, or done wrongly.

This is a research study.

  • You will watch 27 short, silent videos (or pairs of videos) and answer yes/no questions about each one. It takes about 20 minutes. Please use a desktop or laptop computer.
  • Participation is voluntary. You can withdraw at any time by closing this page and returning the study on Prolific.
  • Your responses are anonymous and will be used for an academic research paper.

By clicking I agree you confirm that you are 18 or older and agree to take part.
