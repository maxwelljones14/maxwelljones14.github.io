Which edit keeps the rest of the video unchanged?

What you will judge

Each task shows an edit instruction and two AI edits side by side. Each column shows a video before the edit (top) and the edited video made from it (bottom). The two top videos can differ a little: always compare an edited video with the video directly above it.

Answer one question: Which video better preserves the rest of the scene (everything the edit should not change)? The room, the object's look, the camera and the parts of the motion the instruction does not mention should stay as they were. You must pick one video: if they look alike, pick the one that is even slightly better.

This is a research study.

  • You will watch 22 pairs of short, silent AI-edited videos (each next to the video it was edited from) and pick, for each pair, the one that better keeps everything the edit should not change. It takes about 18 minutes. Please use a desktop or laptop computer.
  • Participation is voluntary. You can withdraw at any time by closing this page and returning the study on Prolific.
  • Your responses are anonymous and will be used for an academic research paper.

By clicking I agree you confirm that you are 18 or older and agree to take part.
