Does the video apply the edit?

What you will judge

Each task shows an edit instruction, a video before the edit and the edited video (an AI's attempt to apply the instruction to it). Answer one question: Does this video apply the edit?

Check the specific change the instruction asks for: count the repetitions, and check the path, direction, speed and where the motion ends. Answer Yes only if the edited video clearly makes that change. Answer No if the change is missing, only partly done or done wrongly (e.g. the wrong number of repetitions, the wrong direction or path).

This is a research study.

  • You will watch 30 short, silent AI-edited videos (each next to the video it was edited from) and answer whether each one applies the requested edit. It takes about 20 minutes. Please use a desktop or laptop computer.
  • Participation is voluntary. You can withdraw at any time by closing this page and returning the study on Prolific.
  • Your responses are anonymous and will be used for an academic research paper.

By clicking I agree you confirm that you are 18 or older and agree to take part.
