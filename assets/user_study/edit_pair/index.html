Which video applies the edit better?

What you will judge

Each task shows an edit instruction and two AI edits side by side. Each column shows a video before the edit (top) and the edited video made from it (bottom). The two top videos can differ a little: always compare an edited video with the video directly above it.

1. Which video better applies the edit? Check the specific change the instruction asks for: count the repetitions, and check the path, direction, speed and where the motion ends. Answer Tie if both apply it equally well, Neither if neither video applies the edit.

2. Which video better preserves the rest of the scene (everything the edit should not change)? The room, the object's look, the camera and the parts of the motion the instruction does not mention should stay as they were. Answer Tie if both keep them equally well.

This is a research study.

  • You will watch 18 pairs of short, silent AI-edited videos (each next to the video it was edited from) and answer two comparison questions about each pair. It takes about 20 minutes. Please use a desktop or laptop computer.
  • Participation is voluntary. You can withdraw at any time by closing this page and returning the study on Prolific.
  • Your responses are anonymous and will be used for an academic research paper.

By clicking I agree you confirm that you are 18 or older and agree to take part.
