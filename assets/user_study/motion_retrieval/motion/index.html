Which motion looks more natural?

What you will judge

Each task shows two AI-generated videos of the same scene side by side, playing in sync. Both show the same animal or object moving. Pick the video in which its movement looks more natural and physically plausible:

  • the body moves the way the real animal or object would (legs, wings or fins in a believable rhythm, no gliding or sliding);
  • believable weight, balance and contact with the floor and other objects;
  • nothing floats, teleports, passes through things or bends impossibly.

A second question asks which video better shows the requested motion (shown above the videos). Judge the motion, not image quality. You must pick one video: if they look alike, pick the slightly better one.

This is a research study.

  • You will watch 19 pairs of short, silent videos and pick the better video of each pair for two questions. It takes about 16 minutes. Please use a desktop or laptop computer.
  • Participation is voluntary. You can withdraw at any time by closing this page and returning the study on Prolific.
  • Your responses are anonymous and will be used for an academic research paper.

By clicking I agree you confirm that you are 18 or older and agree to take part.
